### Provenance limits

The homogeneous and piston datasets retain the archived single-phase results. The force data reach the archived integrated-force tables and postprocessing rather than a new solver rerun. Both area definitions are algebraically identical in these single-fluid domains. The two-phase comparisons explicitly use the stated area selections. Figure 1 is an original TikZ/PGF construction with its CC-BY-4.0 license recorded in the dataset. Source paths in `origins.json` describe collection history only; all data needed to reproduce numerical figures and tables are contained in the stable data publication directory.
